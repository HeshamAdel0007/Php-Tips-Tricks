## 📌 Why Follow Best Practices?

Following best practices ensures that your PHP code:
- ✅ Is **easy to read** and **understand** by other developers (and your future self).
- 🧪 Is **less error-prone** and easier to test/debug.
- 🔄 Promotes **reusability** and **modularity**.
- 🔐 Helps prevent **security vulnerabilities**.
- 🧼 Complies with modern **PHP standards** (like PSR-12).
